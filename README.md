# NGU-GameOn-Documentation

[![CC BY-NC-SA 4.0][cc-by-nc-sa-shield]][cc-by-nc-sa]

**NGU GameOn** is a non-commercial video project created by the NGU Team in collaboration with the MPAM Laboratory, directly paying homage to the QuizKnock YouTube Channel. Our aim is to delve into intriguing aspects of Rhythm Games. This documentation repository serves as a detailed record and archive, including:

- **Pre-production scripts** and publishable **episode proposals**
- **Production scripts and notifications** for our casts
- **Post-production scripts**, timelines and on-screen **packaging assets**
- **Thumbnails** and assets for our **short-form videos**

**NGU GameOn** is designed as an episode-limited series. Our production workflow is structured as follows: **Pre-production** → **Rolling** → **Post-production** → **Publish**. The **Publish** stage indicates the completion of all production processes.

Please note that copyrighted materials, such as music and images, will not be included in this repository.

More on our homepage: 

https://MPAM-Lab.xyz/

## File Structure

- **`ep1-GuessWiki/`** — everything for Episode 1 ("GuessWiki").
  - `NGU-GameOn-ep1-Timeline.drp` — the DaVinci Resolve project/timeline archive.
  - `Questions/` — per-question scripts and answer notes (`1.txt`–`5.txt`, `test.txt`).
  - `images/` — post-production graphics: thumbnails (`封面-16by9.png`, `封面-4by3.png`) and the `包装/` folder of on-screen packaging assets (rendered PNG/MP4 plus Affinity `.af`/`.afdesign`/`.afphoto` sources, including the `words/` character-by-character reveal frames).
- **`ep6-NepLeague/`** — everything for Episode 6 ("NepLeague").
  - `NGU-GameOn-rp6-Timeline.drt` — the DaVinci Resolve timeline export.
  - `包装/` — on-screen packaging assets: question cards (`题目-*.png`), note examples (`note示例.af`, `其他类似note.png`), the "中二玩家" segment graphics, and the outro — as rendered PNGs plus Affinity `.af`/`.afdesign` sources.
  - `封面/` — four thumbnail variants (`16by9` and `4by3`, two versions each).
- **`可公开企划案/`** — publishable episode proposals: `（ep1）NGU Team 视频企划-3-可公开.docx` and `（ep6）NGU Team 视频企划-6-可公开.docx`.
- **`短视频素材/`** — assets for short-form videos, grouped per short: `sr3-ep1-太鼓之达人/` and `sr3-ep2-小岛秀夫/` (rendered PNGs plus Affinity sources).
- **`通用包装素材/`** — reusable packaging assets shared across episodes: maru/batsu marks, rule-of-thirds and quarter guides, borders, the previous-video card, and `达芬奇fusion字幕预设/` (19 DaVinci Resolve Fusion title presets as `.setting` files).
- **`灯射来红 （NGU Team）x MPAM Laboratory 视频选题企划.xlsx`** — the episode planning/topic sheet.
- **`LICENSE`**, **`README.md`**, **`.gitignore`** — repository-level files.

> **Glossary:** `包装` means "packaging" (on-screen graphics/motion assets), `封面` means "thumbnail", and `可公开企划案` means "publishable proposal".

> **Note on original footage**
>
> The original footage (raw camera and gameplay recordings) is **not available to the public** and is **not distributed with this repository**. The `.drp`/`.drt` timelines are shared as a reference for our editing structure; because the media they point to is absent, they will open with offline/unlinked clips. Copyrighted materials, such as music and images, are likewise excluded. Local working folders that are not tracked (see `.gitignore`) may exist on the editors' machines.
